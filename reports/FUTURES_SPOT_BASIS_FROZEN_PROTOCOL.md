# Frozen perpetual trade basis protocol

Frozen before inspecting any new forward outcomes. The companion JSON is the machine-readable authority; neither file may be changed after this freeze. This is an offline information study, not a strategy or trading PnL.

The source is Binance Spot trade-close OHLCV already held by the project and Binance Public Data USD-M perpetual **trade-close** 5m klines already SHA-verified in the prior flow study. This is `perpetual_trade_basis`, not mark/index/premium-index basis. At UTC boundary T, Spot and Futures must both represent the complete interval `[T-timeframe,T)` and close at `T-1ms`; Futures must contain 12/48 complete 5m bars. `basis_pct=100*((futures_close/multiplier)/spot_close-1)`. SHIBUSDT maps to 1000SHIBUSDT, PEPEUSDT to 1000PEPEUSDT, each with multiplier 1000. Six other pairs map directly.

The universe is BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, XRP, DOGE, SHIB, PEPE versus USDT on 1h and 4h. Discovery is 2024, validation 2025, 4h 2026 through 2026-09-30 is diagnostic only. 1h stops at 2025-12-31 because 2026 Spot 1h is absent. Feature definitions B1–B5, exact H1–H4 masks, 2024-only quantile thresholds, Spot-forward targets, primary horizons 6 bars (1h) and 3 bars (4h), and A/B/C models are specified in the JSON. No threshold search, funding or taker flow in primary comparison.

Main formal comparison: per-symbol/timeframe Ridge(alpha=10), fit and standardize on discovery only, test on identical complete rows; A has 14 Spot+Futures OHLCV features from previous A2, B has five basis features, C=A+B. Signed Spot-forward MSE uplift `(A-C)/A` is primary; H4 uses absolute-return MSE. H1/H2/H3 test signed residual from A, H4 tests absolute residual. Weekly block bootstrap (500, seed 20261028), eight-test Holm correction, per-quarter/symbol/leave-one-out/outlier checks. Event cost hurdles 0/0.30/0.60 percentage points do not imply executable PnL.

No future data may enter features, quantiles or training. Forward outcomes must stay inside each split. Missing inputs are excluded on identical rows for A/B/C; no filling. Extreme values are investigated, not auto-deleted. Prior market history was already viewed, so these are internal chronological checks rather than a pristine external holdout. PASS classes and overall YES/NO/INCONCLUSIVE are fixed in the JSON. No production or PAPER change, orders, DeepSeek request, commit or push.
